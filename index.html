The 5 Steps

1. OBJECT — What is the thing?

Name it in one sentence. No scope creep.

2. STATE — What do I have right now?

Time, energy, tools, mood. Be honest.

3. FRICTION — What blocks motion?

Perfectionism? Fear? Not knowing the tool? Name the smallest version.

4. MOTION — What is the next executable action?

Not the plan. The NEXT action. Timebox to 15 minutes.

5. REFINEMENT — What did I learn?

After execution: what worked? What didn't? Update your system.
